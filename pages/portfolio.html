The Creative Ritual.

A deep dive into the alchemical process behind the works.

01

Sourcing Light

Thorne begins every piece by capturing raw environmental data—light wavelengths, ambient sounds, and local geographic textures.

02

Geometric Synthesis

The raw data is processed through custom algorithms, creating the complex geometric structures that serve as the piece's digital skeleton.

03

Archival Hardening

Final works are hardened for longevity—either through high-end archival printing or permanent on-chain verification.
